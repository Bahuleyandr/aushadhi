## Approved management

The typed dispensing action is `confirm_and_monitor`. The alert requires the
prescriber or anticoagulation service to direct whether warfarin dose adjustment
is needed and to arrange PT/INR monitoring during concomitant oral
clarithromycin use, with a clinician-directed follow-up decision when the course
ends. The pharmacy is not instructed to change a dose or stop either medicine
independently. The alert includes bleeding-symptom counselling and advice not to
stop warfarin without consulting a healthcare professional. Erythromycin and
every other macrolide, combipacks, 500 mg presentations, oral suspensions,
injections, and every other unreviewed or non-tablet presentation are excluded;
no universal PT/INR schedule or fixed post-discontinuation interval is asserted.
