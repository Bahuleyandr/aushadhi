## Approved management

The typed dispensing action is `confirm_and_monitor`. The alert requires
prescriber/anticoagulation-service warfarin dose review and prescriber-directed
PT/INR monitoring when voriconazole is started or stopped. The pharmacy is not
instructed to change a dose, stop either medicine independently, or mandate
substitution to another antifungal. The alert includes bleeding-symptom
counselling. Intravenous and other non-tablet voriconazole presentations are
excluded; no universal PT/INR schedule or fixed post-discontinuation interval is
asserted.
