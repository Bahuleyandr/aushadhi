## Required edits (per row)

### A
- **1 (E):** keep Major; not "add GI protection" automatically. Avoid systemic NSAIDs where possible; if intentionally prescribed → lowest dose/shortest course, assess gastroprotection, check INR after start AND stop, counsel bleeding. PPI reduces upper-GI injury but NOT systemic bleeding risk.
- **3 (E):** split by azole, route, formulation, jurisdiction. Miconazole oral/oromucosal gel is a strong rule; UK OTC vs Rx wording differs (may be contraindicated or very close INR-guided). Do NOT expand to topical skin azoles.
- **4 (E):** replace "prefer azithromycin" → "prefer an antibiotic without the relevant interaction where clinically appropriate" (azithromycin still alters anticoagulation + has QT considerations). Require INR plan after start AND stop.
- **10 (E):** consider Major not Moderate (reports of markedly ↑INR, severe/fatal bleeding). Alt analgesic where feasible; else INR after start + stop + urgent bleeding advice.
- **13 (E):** split apixaban vs rivaroxaban. Not "avoid" for every case. US apixaban: 5/10 mg BID + combined strong CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitor → reduce 50%; on 2.5 mg BID → avoid. Rivaroxaban differs.
- **14 (E):** split by DOAC. Apixaban/rivaroxaban = combined P-gp + strong CYP3A induction; dabigatran/edoxaban = primarily P-gp victims. No single class expression for all DOACs.
- **15 (E):** conditional on indication/renal/perpetrator/jurisdiction. Dronedarone/ketoconazole/verapamil/amiodarone/clarithromycin differ. US dabigatran NVAF: renal-specific dose adjustment for selected P-gp inhibitors; VTE differs.
- **16 (E):** keep omeprazole/esomeprazole warning; replace "or H2 blocker" → "an acid suppressant with little/no CYP2C19 inhibition" (cimetidine is NOT a generic-safe H2 alternative).
- **17 (E):** timing applies principally to IR low-dose aspirin + ibuprofen (esp. single dose). Not reliable for EC aspirin, repeated ibuprofen, other NSAIDs. Keep a separate additive-GI-risk warning.
- **18 (E):** replace "avoid inadvertent overlap" → confirm indication + planned duration; do NOT independently stop prescribed antithrombotic; urgently escalate unplanned/unexplained overlap; assess bleeding risk + gastroprotection. Triple therapy may be intentional, time-limited.

### B
- **25 (E):** simvastatin cap with verapamil/diltiazem = **10 mg/day** (US), not "10–20". Jurisdiction/version this.
- **27 (E):** perpetrator-specific. Atorvastatin limits vary by inhibitor + boosted ARV; not "limit/avoid" generic. Includes 20 mg/day limit with some, avoidance with selected regimens.
- **28 (E):** split by statin. Ciclosporin → avoid for one, low cap for another, differing management.
- **30 (E):** split by statin + exact ARV/booster. Simva/lova often contraindicated; atorva/rosuva regimen-dependent caps; prava/pitava differ.

### C
- **31 (E):** split psychiatric MAOIs from linezolid + IV methylene blue. Washout is direction/agent-specific; urgent linezolid has a supervised exception (stop serotonergic promptly + monitor). Fluoxetine longer pre-MAOI interval.
- **32 (E):** management too weak for Major. Prefer non-serotonergic analgesic; assess seizure history + total serotonergic burden; educate urgent SS/seizure symptoms; prescriber review before a new combination.
- **34 (E):** no universal pre-treatment washout when linezolid urgently needed. Encode labeled urgent-use path (no alternative → stop serotonergic promptly → monitor during + post period → generally don't restart until ≥24 h after last linezolid).
- **35 (E):** triptan-member-specific (not all rely on MAO-A equally). Too broad for auto class expansion.
- **37 (E):** split: DXM+MAOI = Contra/avoid (+ post-MAOI interval); DXM+SSRI/SNRI = risk-dependent (dose, misuse, other serotonergics).
- **38 (E):** boxed-warning language: reserve for inadequate alternatives; lowest doses/shortest duration; counsel + monitor sedation/resp depression. NOT "avoid co-dispensing"; do not imply unilateral pharmacist stop of specialist combo.
- **39 (E):** add risk modifiers (age, renal, COPD/respiratory disease, opioid dose, other CNS depressants); lowest gabapentinoid dose + monitor.
- **42 (R):** remove as DDI row. Tyramine = food–drug; indirect sympathomimetics duplicate 41. Merge named drugs into 41; keep tyramine as separate food/counselling rule.
- **43 (E):** delete vague "lithium level shifts"; principal concern is serotonin toxicity. Monitor lithium only when clinically indicated (renal/Na/fluid/illness or a real PK interaction).

### D (all E — risk-tier, not blanket)
- **46 (E):** replace universal Major with a risk-tier model: explicit contraindication? drug-specific TdP risk? route/dose? baseline QTc, hypoK/hypoMg, bradycardia, HF, renal/hepatic accumulation, sex/age, number of agents. Labels differ for citalopram/pimozide/domperidone/ondansetron.
- **47 (E):** azithromycin not QT-safe default. "Prefer non-QT antibiotic where appropriate"; else ECG/electrolyte + patient risk. Keep clarithromycin CYP interactions separate.
- **48 (E):** separate citalopram/escitalopram; jurisdiction-version max doses. US citalopram 20 mg/day max in higher-exposure groups; generally avoid with other QT drugs.
- **49 (E):** domperidone + QT drugs or potent CYP3A4 inhibitors = **Contra** (EMA/UK); include adult max dose + short duration where required.
- **50 (E):** split: ondansetron + apomorphine = **Contra** (profound hypotension/LOC), not primarily QT; other QT drugs → risk-based ECG/electrolyte.
- **51 (E):** split haloperidol by route/dose; separate ziprasidone. IV/high-dose haloperidol ≠ oral maintenance.
- **52 (E):** split PD QT stacking from PK inhibition ↑methadone; ECG management by dose/baseline QTc/risk; not all CYP inhibitors equal.
- **53 (E):** patient-risk logic; HCQ + another QT drug not uniformly Major; assess baseline QT/electrolytes/bradycardia/structural heart disease/renal-hepatic/2nd agent TdP risk.

### E
- **54 (E):** may be intentional specialist combo. Verify indication; assess HR/BP/conduction/LV function; monitor; escalate new/unexplained. No blanket non-dispensing.
- **55 (E):** add coadministration bradycardia risk. Withdrawal: don't stop clonidine abruptly; withdraw beta-blocker several days before tapering clonidine (prescriber-directed).
- **56 (E):** split verapamil/amiodarone/dronedarone. Baseline digoxin level + perpetrator-specific dose/frequency reduction + level/renal/HR/toxicity monitoring.
- **58 (E):** expand to loop/thiazide hypoK OR hypoMg + renal function; use actual/recent electrolytes where available, not every digoxin-diuretic pair Major.
- **59 (E):** split strong-CYP3A inhibition (avoid/contra for ivabradine) from QT stacking (separate bradycardia/QT assessment).
- **60 (E):** procedural/administration interaction, not outpatient co-dispensing; notify administering team; lower adenosine dose (dipyridamole potentiates).

### F
- **61 (E):** ACEi/ARB + MRA can be guideline-directed. Replace "avoid if renal impairment" with baseline + follow-up K⁺/eGFR + product/guideline thresholds. Split spironolactone/eplerenone from amiloride.
- **62 (E):** avoid UNPRESCRIBED K⁺ supplements + salt substitutes; prescribed K⁺ may be intentional but needs indication + monitoring.
- **63 (E):** split 2-drug from triple-whammy. ACEi/ARB+NSAID = conditional renal risk; ↑ with diuretic/dehydration/older age/CKD. Hydration + renal assessment, not blanket "avoid NSAID."
- **66 (R):** duplicate of 65 → merge as high-risk member (spironolactone + trimethoprim/co-trimoxazole).
- **71 (R):** too broad, not pair-specific. Replace with explicit pairs and/or a separate contextual cumulative-AKI engine.
- **72 (E):** reserve Major for high-dose MTX or significant accumulation risk. Low-dose weekly MTX + stable NSAID may be used under supervision. Spacing does NOT reliably prevent impaired clearance.
- **74 (E):** oncology/high-dose-MTX conditional rule (generally Major when protocol-relevant); not a general Moderate PPI alert for low-dose weekly MTX.

### G

### I (all E)

### J
