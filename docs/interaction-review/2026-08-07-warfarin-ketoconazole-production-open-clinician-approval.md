## Approved management

The typed dispensing action is `confirm_and_monitor`. The alert requires
prescriber/anticoagulation-service warfarin dose review and PT/INR monitoring
when oral ketoconazole is started or stopped. The pharmacy is not instructed to
change a dose or stop either medicine independently. The alert includes
bleeding-symptom counselling. Ketoconazole soap and every other topical or
non-tablet presentation is excluded; no universal PT/INR schedule or fixed
post-discontinuation interval is asserted.
