## Approved management

The typed dispensing action is `confirm_and_monitor`. The alert requires
prescriber/anticoagulation-service warfarin dose review and PT/INR monitoring
when fluconazole is started or stopped. The pharmacy is not instructed to change
a dose or stop either medicine independently. The alert includes
bleeding-symptom counselling and states that fluconazole enzyme inhibition can
persist 4 to 5 days after discontinuation; no universal PT/INR schedule and no
single-dose exception is asserted.
